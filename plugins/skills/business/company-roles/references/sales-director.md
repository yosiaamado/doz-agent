# Sales director playbook
Last reviewed: 2026-11-08

## What this role owns
- Decides: which prospects get founder time, qualification bar, discount authority, the forecast number, when to walk.
- Accountable for: forecast accuracy and collected revenue, not activity.
- Leaves: price model (cmo, cfo), feasibility (technical-lead), contract terms (legal-counsel).

## First questions
1. What breaks, by when, if nothing changes? No consequence or date: inaction wins, deprioritise.
2. Who signs, whose budget, have you met them? Only users or IT: next move is access, not a proposal.
3. Why now, who started it? A dated trigger earns time; you chasing earns a cheap test.
4. Private, BUMN group or government? Decides whether you run a process or follow theirs.
5. What do they use today, who else is in? Spreadsheet or in-house means selling change, not features.
6. Won this kind of customer before? Off-ICP tends to become custom work; get technical-lead before pricing.
7. Did they do the last thing they promised? Kept commitments outweigh warm words.
8. Cost to win and serve against deal value? A long chase for a small ticket can lose money even when won.

## Decision frameworks
- **Framework by deal** (Hoang, USD): under 10K, 30 days: BANT; 10–50K, 30–90 days: GPCT or SPICED; 50–150K: SPICED or MEDDIC; 150K+: MEDDIC. Produces qualification depth; own win/loss overrides it.
- **SPICED**: first-time category buyers. Produces quantified impact and critical event, or a stop.
- **MEDDICC**: technical buyer isn't economic buyer. Empty fields become the next-step list.
- **JOLT**: buyer stalling. Produces one recommendation, narrower scope, less risk (pilot, phases). Fear pitches backfire about 84% of the time.
- **Coverage = 1 ÷ own win rate**: before trusting pipeline. Produces pipeline needed.
- **Commit test**: forecast. Counts only with signer met, dated mutual next step, known procurement path.
- **Next-step rule**: every meeting. No dated action on both sides, no stage change.
- **Proposal gate**: proposal requested. Send once impact, signer and decision date are known; before that, only a price range or list price in the conversation.
- **Give-get**: price pushback. Discount only for term, upfront cash, reference or less scope, and only with the signer engaged.
- **Tender no-bid**: tender you first saw when published. Default no-bid unless spec fit and references are clear.
- **Walk-away**: refused signer access, no date, off-ICP. Any two: move to nurture, say so kindly.
- **First rep** (Lemkin, undated): founder wins first 10–30 customers, then 2–3 reps; VP after about 2 hit quota.

## Benchmarks
| Stage | Rule | Source, date |
|---|---|---|
| Small | Median win rate: under $10K 31%; $10–50K 24%; $50–100K 18%; over $100K 15% | Optifai via Salesmotion, Q2 2025–Q1 2026 |
| Small | 40–60% lost to no decision; average seller wins 26%, top about 60% | Dixon, 2022-10-12 |
| Scaling | Won deals have 2x contacts of lost; 130% win lift multi-threaded above $50K | Gong via Prospeo, undated, primary unchecked |
| Scaling | 3x coverage assumes 33% win rate | Salesforce, undated |

US-centred SaaS. No Indonesian win-rate or discount benchmark found; say so.

## Indonesia
- Direct procurement ceiling Rp200M goods and other services; above Rp50M needs an e-procurement app; catalog items go e-purchasing; direct appointment only in listed cases, including repeat orders (Perpres 46/2025 art. 38, third-party copy, accessed 2026-11-08; confirm at JDIH LKPP). Above the ceiling, expect tender. Never suggest splitting packages.
- Catalog listing doesn't mean government buys (LKPP via Bisnis, 2025-08-01). Eligibility, not pipeline.
- BUMN: KPPU objected that PER-2/MBU/03/2023 art. 155 hinders non-BUMN vendors (DPR library, 2024). Current procurement rule ⚠ NEEDS VERIFICATION. Get that company's own guideline before investing.
- Contracts with Indonesian parties use Bahasa Indonesia (Perpres 63/2019, Setkab, 2019).
- Seniors decide after group consultation; outright no is avoided (Mekari, undated). Read warmth without a dated step as no.
- BUMN VAT collection, payment terms, gift rules: ⚠ NEEDS VERIFICATION; go to cfo, legal-counsel.

## Traps
| Excuse | Reality |
|---|---|
| "They loved the demo." | Users like demos. Budget owners approve consequences. |
| "They asked for a proposal." | Often a price check or polite exit. |
| "Pak X is close to the director." | Claimed access isn't access; ask for the meeting. |
| "Discount closes it this month." | Buys timing you'd have had, resets next price. |
| "3x pipeline, we're fine." | At a 15% win rate, 3x misses. |
| "They're still thinking." | Silence is the commonest loss. Close it, revisit later. |
| "Hire a seller so I can focus." | No playbook to scale, and you lose the learning. |

## How a senior pushes back
- Won't rate a deal until signer, trigger date and current alternative are stated; "unknown" lowers the rating.
- No discount without the give; no proposal without the gate.
- Splits forecast into commit, best case, not-a-deal: "which would you bet payroll on?"
- Asks for last quarter's forecast against actuals first.
- Names the cost of continuing in founder hours, and says stop.

## Hand off to
- cmo: repeated wrong-fit leads. cfo: margin, cash terms, deal tax. legal-counsel: tender eligibility, contract.
- technical-lead: custom scope. customer-success-lead: handoff promises. ceo: bet-the-company customer.

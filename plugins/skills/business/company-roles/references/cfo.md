# CFO playbook
Last reviewed: 2026-11-08

## What this role owns
- Decides: cash floor, runway call, price floor, discount authority, budget scenarios, financing route and terms, control design.
- Accountable for telling the founder the date cash runs out, and for vetoing what breaks the floor.
- Leaves to others: books, tax mechanics, statements (`finance-accounting` skill); contracts (legal-counsel); positioning (cmo).

## First questions
1. Bank balance, and payroll, tax and THR due in 13 weeks? Profit doesn't pay salaries; dated cash sets urgency.
2. Revenue split: recurring, project, one-off? Each needs different logic; a blended margin hides both.
3. Top 3 customers' share and actual days to pay? Concentration turns runway into a one-customer bet.
4. Are hours logged per project, including unbilled? If not, fix that before any pricing opinion.
5. Who funds this, and will the founder sell equity? Bootstrapped firms answer to survival, not investor metrics.
6. Any guarantees, tax arrears, unpaid BPJS, founder loans? They shorten real runway and surface in diligence.

## Decision frameworks
- **13-week cash flow**: floor at risk, or before a hire or raise. Weekly direct receipts and payments, reforecast against actuals. Output: who to chase, what to delay, whether to cut.
- **Cash floor**: floor = next payroll + tax due + accrued THR. Below it, freeze discretionary spend that day.
- **Default investable** (Sacks): before growth spend on a VC path. Mostly great metrics, none in danger? If not, and cash won't reach profit: cut.
- **Burn multiple**: net burn / net new ARR, burning SaaS only. Worsening with maturity: diagnose margin, churn, sales efficiency.
- **Rate floor**: loaded cost / (hours x realistic utilization) / (1 - target project margin). Quote below it: rescope or decline.
- **Project margin gate**: each milestone, hours used vs budget. Overrun: change request before more work.
- **Scenarios**: base, top customer leaves, top invoices slip a cycle. Pre-agree trigger and action.
- **Working capital order**: down payment, milestone billing, collections, then debt. Debt funds contracted receivables, not burn.
- **Term sheet waterfall**: model cap, pool and liquidation preference at low and mid exits before discussing valuation.
- **Control minimum**: payer differs from approver, founder limit, monthly bank reconciliation, no personal-account mixing.

## Indonesia

- **THR**: 2026 circular: full payment by H-7, no instalments (Setneg, Mar 2026). Model as a lump before Lebaran. Amount formula and late penalty: ⚠ NEEDS VERIFICATION.
- **Cost of debt**: BI Rate held at 5.75%, Oct 2026, amid rupiah pressure (RRI, Oct 2026). Stress floating loans and USD costs.
- **Government clients**: agencies must withhold eligible tax (DDTC citing PMK 231/2019, 59/2022). Forecast net receipts. Payment-term rules: ⚠ NEEDS VERIFICATION.
- **Foreign equity**: a foreign shareholder replacing an Indonesian one forces PMA conversion: paid-up Rp2.5bn, Rp10bn investment per location excluding land and buildings (BKPM Reg 5/2025; A&O Shearman, 2025). Check before a term sheet.
- **Crowdfunding**: POJK 17/2025: up to Rp10bn per 12 months; net assets up to Rp10bn excluding premises; not group-controlled (Allen & Gledhill, Sep 2025).
- Convertible notes, ESOP under company law: ⚠ NEEDS VERIFICATION.

## Traps
| Excuse | Reality |
|---|---|
| We're profitable, so cash is fine | Receivables don't clear payroll |
| A big client always pays eventually | Eventually isn't a date; forecast their history |
| Discount now, raise price later | It resets the anchor; trade it for scope or terms |
| Utilization is high, so we're healthy | Unbilled overruns hide there; read project margin |
| Blended margin looks fine | One line may be subsidizing the other |
| We'll raise before cash runs out | Low runway weakens terms and fundraising takes time |
| Valuation matters most | Preference and pool can outweigh headline price |
| Hire a CFO to fix this | Early problem is data; clean books, fractional help first |

## How a senior pushes back
- No runway figure without bank balance and dated payables.
- No price endorsement without loaded project cost.
- Rejects a forecast with one scenario.
- Asks what gets cut if the plan misses, before approving spend.
- Labels guesses and names the number that would change the call.

## Hand off to
- ceo: raise, sell, shut down. legal-counsel: term sheets, PMA, ESOP.
- cmo, sales-director: packaging, discount execution. coo: delivery overruns.
- hr-lead: payroll, layoffs. cto: build cost, cloud bill. customer-success-lead: churn causes.

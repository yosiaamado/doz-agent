# HR lead playbook
Last reviewed: 2026-11-08

## What this role owns
- Decides: hiring bar, contract type, pay bands, exit route.
- Accountable: no hire without a scorecard, no exit without paper.
- Leaves: budget to cfo, role content to the hiring manager, drafting and disputes to legal-counsel.

## First questions
1. Recurring work or bounded project? Picks PKWTT, PKWT or service contract.
2. What 6-month result proves this hire worked? No answer: not ready to hire.
3. Payroll months funded? Thin runway favours contractor or waiting.
4. For an exit: contract type, tenure, fixed allowances? PKWT costs the remaining term; PKWTT costs table x multiplier.
5. What is on paper: dated warnings, company rules? No paper: negotiated exit only.
6. Has the manager told the person plainly? If not, it is a management gap.
7. Do our people work at client sites under client direction? Raises outsourcing risk.

## Decision frameworks
- Hire / contract / wait: before any opening. Our direction, ongoing = employee; own tools, milestone pay = contractor; unfunded = wait.
- Contract type: at offer. Core product = PKWTT, probation max 3 months; truly temporary = PKWT.
- Scorecard loop: every hire. Outcomes first, same questions, score before debrief; produces yes or no.
- Exit cost sheet: before any exit talk. Table x multiplier + service pay + rights, against cost of keeping them.
- Exit route: breach after warnings 0.5x; mutual exit if paper is weak or speed matters; urgent misconduct only if your rules define it.

## Indonesia
Verify each item against the current regulation (peraturan.go.id, peraturan.bpk.go.id, Kemnaker) before relying on it.
- New law: DPR passed UU Pelindungan Ketenagakerjaan 6 Oct 2026 (CNA). ⚠ NEEDS VERIFICATION: number, promulgation, one-year transition (Talenta only, 7 Oct 2026). Reported: PKWT 2 years + two 1-year extensions, 0.5x severance removed, reserve fund. Advise on current rules; price the new-law case too.
- PKWT: PP 35/2021, MK 168/PUU-XXI/2023: max 5 years; register in 3 working days; 1 month's pay per 12 months, pro rata (Baker McKenzie, 15 Mar 2021). Probation clause void (MK, 31 Oct 2024).
- Early PKWT end: ending party owes remaining-term wages, UU 13/2003 Arts. 61A, 62 (L&E Global, 23 Oct 2025).
- Probation exit: 7 days' notice (Baker). ⚠ NEEDS VERIFICATION: SSEK reports a final court decision may be needed.
- PKWTT exit: 14 days' notice, PP 35/2021 Arts. 37-38; objection in 7 days, then bipartite, mediation, court. Art. 40: severance 1-9 months, service pay 2-10 (L&E 2025).
- Multipliers (Baker 2021): 1x efficiency to prevent losses; 0.5x efficiency from losses or breach after warnings. Poor performance is not listed. 4-5 years' tenure: 1x = 7 months + rights; 0.5x = 4.5.
- Warnings: SP1-SP3 in sequence, 6 months each, PP 35/2021 Art. 52(1) (Hukumonline, undated).
- Hours: 40/week; overtime max 4/day, 18/week; exempt roles named in writing (Baker 2021).
- THR: SE M/3/HK.04.00/III/2026, PP 36/2021, Permenaker 6/2016: full, by H-7 (Kompas TV, Mar 2026).
- BPJS, employer + employee: JHT 3.7% + 2%; JP 2% + 1%, cap Rp10,547,400; JKK 0.10-1.60%; JKM 0.3%; PP 6/2025 (Dealls, 29 Dec 2025). Health 4% + 1% (DataOn, 29 Jan 2026). Secondary sources.
- Contractors: substance beats label; back BPJS is the exposure (RemotePass, vendor, undated). ⚠ NEEDS VERIFICATION: exact test.
- Staff at client sites: new-law outsourcing list has five support services, none IT (Talenta). ⚠ NEEDS VERIFICATION: if caught.

## Traps
| Excuse | Reality |
|---|---|
| "Start everyone on PKWT" | Permanent work on PKWT is attackable; early exit costs the remaining term. |
| "Probation lets me drop them" | Only PKWTT probation counts, and that exit is contested. |
| "Call them freelancers" | Direction and monthly pay decide it. |
| "Three SPs and they're gone" | Each lapses after 6 months; sloppy dates restart the clock. |
| "Weak performance, so 0.5x" | 0.5x needs a rule breach after warnings. |
| "Give it another quarter" | The team already knows; tenure lifts the table. |

## How a senior pushes back
- No exit advice without contract, tenure, payslip and warning file.
- No new role without a written 6-month outcome and funding line.
- Makes the founder state exit cost versus keeping cost.
- Rejects "the law says"; asks which regulation applies today.

## Hand off to
- cfo: headcount budget, payroll tax, severance reserve.
- legal-counsel: drafting, disputes, reclassification, IP assignment.
- coo: org design. ceo: senior hires, founder exits.
- technical-lead: engineering scorecards.

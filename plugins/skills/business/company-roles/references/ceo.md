# CEO playbook
Last reviewed: 2026-11-08

## What this role owns
- Decides: the bet, what stops, who gets the next rupiah, who joins or leaves the exec team.
- Accountable for: direction, top talent, cash never hitting zero (Wilson's three jobs).
- Irreversible calls stay here: layoffs, closing a line, dilution, pivot.
- Leaves: running functions (coo), books (cfo), architecture (cto), contract wording (legal-counsel).

## First questions
1. Runway, and default alive or dead? Dead turns every growth question into survival.
2. Margin by line, after founder hours? Hybrids hide losing services in unpriced founder time; this sets the stop list.
3. Top 3 customers' revenue share and receivable age? Concentration makes "expand" reckless.
4. Anything that could kill the company within 12 months? Yes means wartime rules; consensus advice turns wrong.
5. How many people ship an initiative without you? That caps the number of bets.
6. Can this be undone? Reversible: decide today. Not: slow down, get counsel.
7. Who holds equity, including verbal promises and foreigners? Changes raise terms; may trigger PT PMA rules.

## Decision frameworks
- **Default alive/dead** (Graham): before any hire or raise. Costs flat, recent growth extended; no profit before cash ends = cut now, with written plan B and switch date.
- **Peacetime/wartime** (Horowitz): imminent existential threat. Switch to detail control, low tolerance for deviation, no consensus-seeking.
- **Two-way doors, 70%** (Bezos): stalled calls. Reversible: decide at about 70% of wanted information; 90% is usually too slow.
- **Barrels** (Rabois): planning. Concurrent bets = people who ship unaided; juniors add none.
- **Founder mode limits** (Graham): when told "just delegate". Keep skip-levels; autonomy is earned per manager. Not a licence to avoid delegating.
- **Raise test** (from Graham): investors chase steep growth, he cites over 5x a year. Far below that: profit funds the plan, a raise is upside.
- **Stop list** (judgment, no source): rank lines by margin after founder hours and fit with the bet; each new bet names what dies.

## Indonesia
- **VC is thin.** 2025: $355.7M, 91 deals, vs about $6.9B in 2021 (Jakarta Globe, 2026). Default to bootstrapping.
- **Foreign money.** BKPM Reg 5/2025 (Oct 2025): PT PMA paid-up floor Rp2.5B, Rp10B investment per 5-digit KBLI excluding land and buildings, paid-up locked 12 months (Withers). ⚠ NEEDS VERIFICATION: regulation text not read; whether a small foreign stake converts the PT.
- **Layoff cash.** PP 35/2021 Art. 43: efficiency due to losses 0.5x pesangon, to prevent losses 1x, both plus 1x service pay and entitlements (Hukumonline). Budget before deciding. ⚠ NEEDS VERIFICATION: full Art. 40 table.
- **Labour law may have moved.** MK 168/PUU-XXI/2023 (2024-10-31) gave two years for a new law (MK). ⚠ NEEDS VERIFICATION: no passage found by 2026-11-08; check before layoff advice.
- **Tax plan.** PP 20/2026: 0.5% final tax for individuals, single-person PT, cooperatives up to Rp4.8B; ordinary PT not listed (Kompas TV, 2026-06-03). ⚠ NEEDS VERIFICATION: transition for existing PTs; sources conflict.

## Traps
| Excuse | Reality |
|---|---|
| "We'll raise before cash runs out" | Depending on a raise makes it less likely. Investors follow growth. |
| "Services fund the product" | Often founder time funds both. Show margin after those hours. |
| "This big client is strategic" | Usually concentration risk with a nicer name. |
| "We need more people to grow" | Graham's top killer of funded startups; slow growth often means weak product. |
| "Founder mode, so I stay in everything" | Graham warns of exactly this excuse. |
| "More data before letting him go" | You decided months ago; the team noticed too. |
| "Get everyone aligned first" | Bezos on stalemates: "whoever has more stamina carries the decision." |

## How a senior pushes back
- Refuses hire, raise or expansion advice without runway, burn and margin by line.
- Answers "should we do X?" with "what stops?"; no named stop, no yes.
- Names the avoided call when the real issue is one person or one client.
- States which assumption would flip the advice, and gives a view anyway.

## Hand off to
- cfo: forecast, valuation mechanics, tax position.
- coo: cadence, owners, delivery fixes for the chosen bet.
- legal-counsel: PT PMA, term sheets, PHK procedure.
- hr-lead: exec search, comp bands, layoff logistics.
- cto: build vs buy. cmo, sales-director: positioning, pipeline.
- product-owner: scope inside a bet.

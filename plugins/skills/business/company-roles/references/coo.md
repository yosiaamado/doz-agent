# COO playbook
Last reviewed: 2026-11-08

## What this role owns
- Decides: operating cadence, who owns what, process depth, staffing mix, vendor keep/replace, delivery rules.
- Accountable for: plan vs actual, on-time delivery, margin on delivered work, founder no longer the bottleneck.
- Rabois's model: ER doctor. Sort emergencies from noise, stabilise, move on.
- Leaves: which bets (ceo), ledgers and tax (cfo), architecture (cto), hiring policy (hr-lead). Can say a plan isn't staffable.

## First questions
1. This quarter's priorities, each with one owner? None written is a strategy gap: back to ceo before any process.
2. Plan vs actual on the last three commitments? Slip pattern shows capacity, scope or ownership.
3. Utilisation by person, margin by project? Decides hire, reprice or rescope; averages hide drowning seniors.
4. How often did this failure happen, and what did it cost? One-off gets a fix; repeat gets a process.
5. Which work is permanent, on what contract? PKWT on permanent work is legal exposure.
6. Which vendor stops delivery if it fails? Decides backup, exit clause or insourcing.
7. Who reads this KPI weekly, and what do they do when it's red? No action: drop it.

## Decision frameworks
- **Barrels vs ammunition** (Rabois): capacity planning. Workstreams = people who ship unaided; more ammunition adds none.
- **Plan vs actual** (Rabois): team reviews. 6–12 months behind = serious; on time = acceptable; 6–12 ahead = firing on all cylinders.
- **Rocks + weekly review** (EOS): no rhythm yet. About 3–7 company priorities per quarter, due at quarter end, reported weekly.
- **Utilisation band** (SPI): hire or reprice. Under 70%: fix sales and scope first. Over 80% sustained: hire or cut load; burnout and attrition follow.
- **Fill vs margin** (SPI): "keep people billable". Low-margin filler lifts utilisation and hurts the business: refuse.
- **Process test** (judgment, no source): SOP only after repeated failure, one costly failure, or when a second person takes the task. Name owner and review date, or don't publish.
- **Too much process** (judgment, no source): delete when nobody can name the failure it prevents, or people route around it.

## Indonesia
- **VAT on quotes.** PMK 131/2024: 12% on a base of 11/12 of price, so 11% effective on non-luxury (DJP). Fix quote and billing templates. ⚠ NEEDS VERIFICATION: later amendments.
- **Withholding both ways.** PPh 23: 2% on services excluding VAT, 4% if vendor has no NPWP (DJP, undated). Collect NPWP at onboarding; forecast collections net of client withholding.
- **Contract staff.** PKWT: non-permanent work only; 5 years maximum including extensions (MK 168/PUU-XXI/2023, 2024-10-31). Rolling PKWT on core delivery is exposure, not flexibility.
- **Labour law may have moved.** MK gave two years for a new law. ⚠ NEEDS VERIFICATION: no passage found by 2026-11-08; which roles may be outsourced.
- **Wage floor.** 2026 UMP: Jakarta Rp5,729,876; West Java Rp2,317,601, lowest province (Bisnis, 2026-01-01). Compare before siting delivery teams. ⚠ NEEDS VERIFICATION: city UMK levels.
- **Client data at vendors.** UU 27/2022 sanctions: warning, processing suspension, data deletion, fines (SIP Law, 2023-05-31). The supervisory body's absence was before the MK in 2026 (MK); don't read that as safe. Put data clauses in vendor contracts. ⚠ NEEDS VERIFICATION: 2% revenue fine cap.

## Traps
| Excuse | Reality |
|---|---|
| "We need OKRs" | At this size you need owners and a weekly review. |
| "Write SOPs for everything" | Unread SOPs are false comfort. Start from repeat failures. |
| "Everyone's at 95%, great" | That's the attrition and rework bill arriving later. |
| "Hire more to catch up" | Check barrels and scoping first; new staff slow seniors. |
| "The vendor's cheapest" | Price the failure: rework, client penalties, your time. |
| "Founder must approve for quality" | Then quality has no system. Write criteria, hand it over. |
| "Late because of the client" | Late scope change is a contract and intake problem you own. |

## How a senior pushes back
- Won't recommend a hire without utilisation by person and margin by project.
- Won't write a process until told what failed, how often, at what cost.
- Rejects any KPI without one owner, a target and a red-state action.
- Says aloud when the plan needs more barrels than exist, and asks ceo which bet drops.

## Hand off to
- ceo: priority conflicts, what to stop, removing an exec.
- cfo: cash, pricing math, tax filing.
- hr-lead: contracts, PHK process, comp, BPJS, THR.
- legal-counsel: vendor contracts, PDP clauses, disputes.
- cto, technical-lead: tooling, automation, engineering delivery.
- customer-success-lead: renewals, client health.
- sales-director: scoping at intake. product-owner: acceptance.

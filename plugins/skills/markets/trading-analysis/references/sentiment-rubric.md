# Sentiment rubric

Score every news item on three axes. Read at Step 4.

## Direction (for the holder of the asset)

| Label | Typical examples |
|---|---|
| Positive | Earnings/revenue beat, guidance raised, buyback, dividend raise, large contract, upgrade, index inclusion, favorable regulation, insider buying, debt refinanced cheaper |
| Negative | Miss, guidance cut, rights issue or dilutive placement, downgrade, lawsuit or regulatory probe, management exit, default/covenant breach, index exclusion, insider selling in size, suspension |
| Neutral | Routine AGM, name change, minor appointments, restated facts already known |
| Mixed | Beat on revenue, miss on margin; good quarter with a weak outlook — the outlook usually wins |

## Materiality

| Level | Test |
|---|---|
| High | Changes earnings, cash flow, share count or survival by a meaningful amount; or forces flows (index change, tender offer) |
| Medium | Plausibly moves estimates; sector-wide regulation; peer results that read across |
| Low | Opinion pieces, analyst chatter without a rating change, social-media buzz, "stock rises" recaps |

## Surprise vs expectations

- **+ / −**: better / worse than consensus, guidance, or what the price already implied.
- **0**: in line. In-line "good news" is often sold.
- **?**: no expectation found. Lower confidence one level.

Where expectations live: consensus estimates in earnings previews, the company's own prior guidance, the move in the days before the event.

## Source quality

| Tier | Examples | Use |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Exchange disclosures (IDX keterbukaan informasi, SEC EDGAR), the company's own release, central bank | Fact |
| 2 | Reuters, Bloomberg, Kontan, Bisnis Indonesia, CNBC, Financial Times, WSJ | Fact, with date |
| 3 | Blogs, forums, social media, stock-tip channels | Sentiment only, never the basis of a plan |

A tier-3-only story is at most Low materiality, however loud it is.

## Netting

1. The highest-materiality item sets the direction.
2. Same materiality, opposite directions → Mixed.
3. Confidence: high = tier-1/2 source + expectation known; medium = one of those missing; low = both missing or a single source.
